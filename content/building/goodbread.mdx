I'm Chief Credit Officer at GoodBread Innovations, which works to widen microloan access
for underserved entrepreneurs. The work runs out of Kansas City and was recently backed
by a $1.38 million grant to expand that access.

My side of it is credit: reviewing borrower transaction histories, and building the
judgment layer that decides who gets capital and on what terms — for a population that
conventional underwriting is bad at reading.

On the team I'm the second follower. The one who makes it safe for everyone else to
join in.
