A borrower can be current on every payment while the business's cash is heading the
wrong way. By the time a payment is missed, most of the good options are gone.
[CircumFi](https://circumfi.com) watches the months in between.

A lender invites each business owner, and the owner connects their business bank
account through Plaid. CircumFi reads the account activity; it can't move money. While
the account stays connected, CircumFi watches it in real time and gives the lender early
warning of the changes that come before trouble: a new merchant cash advance, falling
revenue, bounced payments, rising debt payments. Then it
routes the borrower to the lender's team while technical assistance, a temporary
deferment, or a modification can still make a difference. The team sets policy and
makes the call. CircumFi gathers the evidence.

The owner gets a free dashboard of their own the whole time, with cash flow insights
and alerts they choose. And the same data works in the other direction: it shows which
borrowers are growing and ready for the next loan.

Lenders have owners, too!
