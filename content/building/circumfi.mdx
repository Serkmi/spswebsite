Small business lenders get offered platforms and told to adapt. CircumFi starts from
the lender's workflow instead, and builds only the piece that's actually broken: IBV
and account aggregation, data standardization, real-time transaction analysis,
underwriting insight, custom cash flow attributes, post-origination account
monitoring — all on the table.

I originally founded CircumFi to help small business owners access low-cost financing
through a process that generated competition among lenders. I do love working with
business owners directly, but now I'm helping lenders remove bottlenecks and find
hidden gems in the applicant pool. Lenders have owners, too!
